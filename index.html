Your phone is the racket. The court waits until it knows which hand you play with, where your arm rests, and that you can see yourself here.

  2. Set your rest position arm relaxed, phone just below the hip
  3. See yourself on the court move the phone and watch the racket below
  4. Start the drill here or on the phone

A jump smash puts the racket at about 3.0 m. Most ceilings are 2.4 m. Rather than drop the stroke, the virtual racket reaches further than the one in your hand — but you can see the figure and you can turn it down.
